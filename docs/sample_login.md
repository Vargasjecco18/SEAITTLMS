# Sample logins

The accounts made by [`database/seaittlms_sample_accounts.sql`](../database/seaittlms_sample_accounts.sql) (1 administrator, 4 staff, 12 teachers and 40 students), with their passwords.
They are **sample accounts for trying the system**: the passwords are easy on purpose and they are written here. **Do not use these accounts or passwords with real people:**
give every real person a new password (Users Management > Edit), or delete the sample accounts.

How they are made, like the app does for a new person:

- staff and teachers: the last name, a dot and the digits of the employee number, for example `villanueva.001@legazpi.seaitt.edu.ph` (employee number T-001);
- students: the last name, a dot and the last 6 digits of the LRN, for example `jacinto.111111@legazpi.seaitt.edu.ph` (LRN 105202111111);
- the password of the staff, the teachers and the students is the last name followed by the same digits (for example `villanueva001`); the administrator keeps `admin@legazpi.seaitt.edu.ph`.

(The administrator of your database is the one the app made from `Seed:Admin` in `appsettings.Local.json`; its password is never changed by the sample files.)
